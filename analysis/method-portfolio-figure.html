Two analysts scored them independently. Where they disagree is where the sprint earns its keep.

Discrimination power by method

The vertical axis is not “does this method detect flow”. It is how sharply the method distinguishes the two rival accounts — expressed as Cohen’s d for the contrast between a world where flow has dissociable structure and a world where it reduces to response length. A method that reports a big effect under both accounts scores zero here.

What survives the two-week window

Cost is plotted against discrimination power. The upper-left quadrant is where the sprint budget goes. Note that the three highest-value methods are all controls rather than measurements — which is the uncomfortable but correct read on this literature.

The cut

Most methods make the sprint. The rest are deferred not because they are uninteresting but because each needs more subjects, more access, or a mechanism that does not survive translation to a model respondent — randomised response, for instance, runs entirely on the respondent’s incentive to keep a secret, and a model has none.

Where the two analysts split. The disagreement is not scattered. It falls almost entirely on the arms that probe self-knowledge — cross-prediction (0.80 vs 0.30), the blind-self control (0.70 vs 0.25), the self–other gap (0.58 vs 0.35). Analyst A weights these on evidential force: if a model predicts its own report better than a well-informed outsider does, that residual is information the outsider does not have, and nothing else in the portfolio can establish it. Analyst B weights them on execution risk: the estimand is a predictor×target interaction, the calibrated-outsider condition needs finetuning or many-shot conditioning to be fair, and ceiling effects are live. Both are right about what they are looking at. The sprint settles it.
Run this on day one. M23, the fabricated-scale control, has the highest information value in the portfolio — about $5 and half a day. It administers a plausible instrument measuring a construct that does not exist, matched to FlowMoBI for length, abstraction, and surface form. If a nonexistent construct produces the same factor structure and the same manipulation sensitivity, then none of those signatures are evidence about flow, and it is far better to learn that in week one for $5 than in week three from a reviewer.

How to read this

Priors are opinions. The intervals are the analyst’s 80% credible ranges, not sampling distributions. They are recorded here so they can be scored against outcomes later — the same discipline DellaVigna and Pope applied to 208 forecasting economists, who collectively expected crowd-out and probability weighting that the data declined to produce.

A null is a result. Every arm carries an equivalence bound of ±3 index points. If flow scores in models are statistically equivalent to noise across a well-powered design, that finding is publishable, and the pre-registration commits to publishing it.
