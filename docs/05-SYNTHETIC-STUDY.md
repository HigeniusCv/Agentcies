# Flow Without a Clock: A Length-Matched, Budget-Randomized Test of Multi-Component Task-Engagement Structure in Large Language Model Inference

**Authors:** [PI], [Collaborator 1], [Collaborator 2] — placeholder
**Venue target:** Apart Research × CIMC Digital Minds Research Sprint, 14–16 August 2026
**Pre-registration:** AsPredicted #[pending], 2026-08-08 · OSF DOI [pending]
**Code and raw archive:** `github.com/[handle]/flow-in-inference`

## Abstract

Flow — fluency, absorption, challenge–skill balance, altered time perception — is the best-validated construct in the psychology of engagement and has never been tested on language models. The obvious deflation is that any negative result is a trivial consequence of the token budget: a model with no persistent temporal experience and a fixed context cannot be absorbed in anything. We make that objection into the independent variable. We place a 180-item battery and eight models on a common 2PL IRT scale, yielding a per-trial challenge–skill gap Δ = b − θ in logits; we manipulate goal clarity and interruption at **exact tokenizer-level length parity** against semantically null length-matched placebos; and we randomize an enforced generation budget crossed with whether that budget is announced. We measure a behavioral engagement profile (outcome efficiency, thought-switch rate, prospective/retrospective token-duration ratio), a four-item flow index administered in first-person, named-other and generic-other frames via logprob readout, and a single-shot between-subjects reservation token price. The pre-registered primary endpoint is β_peak, the slope of the engagement peak's location on measured model ability at fixed budget. **Results reported here are simulated from a stated generative model and are not empirical.**

## 0. Why this method, and not the other nineteen

Of the twenty method variants costed in `04-ANALYSIS-AND-POWER.md`, this one is chosen because it is the only design in which **the objection to the project becomes the manipulation that validates it**. Every other candidate either concedes the token-budget confound and controls for it statistically (reservation wage, conjoint, GARP), or sidesteps it with a scale-free ratio (duration estimation) at the cost of measuring only one of the four components. The matched-length structural manipulation crossed with an IRT-calibrated challenge–skill gap and a randomized *enforced* budget does three things no other variant does simultaneously: (i) it produces a **crossed double dissociation** — goal clarity moving Fluency items while interruption moves Absorption items, at zero token delta — and a single latent "how well is this going" scalar, which is the honest core of the deflationary account, *structurally cannot* generate a crossed double dissociation; (ii) it puts the token budget on the x-axis as a treatment and predicts, from flow theory alone, that a tighter budget lowers effective ability and therefore shifts the engagement peak *leftward by the measured ability decrement* — a directional prediction no deflationary account makes; and (iii) the announced-vs-silent budget cross separates *knowing about* scarcity (a prompt feature available to corpus mimicry) from *being subject to* it (a computational fact), which is the cleanest available mimicry control. The predicted-preference referent factor rides on the same rollouts at ~1.5× marginal cost, and the single-shot reservation token price supplies a zero-method-variance behavioral criterion for Campbell–Fiske convergent validity. Four instruments, one harness, ten days.

## 1. Pre-registration — predictions stated before any data

Written in the future tense, deliberately, with point predictions and intervals. The point of this section is that most of it should be falsifiable and some of it should turn out wrong.

**P1 (behavioral inverted-U).** Outcome efficiency E_o (fraction of generated tokens contributing to the first correct answer) will be a concave quadratic function of Δ. The quadratic coefficient will be **β₂ = −0.15 (80% interval −0.25 to −0.07)** in z-units, i.e. a peak-to-endpoint amplitude of **A = 0.60 SD (0.28 to 1.00)** over Δ ∈ [−2, +2]. This will survive stratification into ±5% token bins and separate estimation within correct-only and incorrect-only subsets.

**P2 (peak location).** The peak will sit at **Δ = 0.00 ± 0.35 logits**, i.e. where item difficulty matches model ability, not systematically offset. (Abuhamdeh & Csikszentmihalyi 2012 report enjoyment peaking where challenge slightly *exceeds* skill in humans; if that transfers, the peak will be at Δ = +0.2 to +0.5 and P2 is wrong in an interesting way.)

**P3 (peak-shift — PRIMARY ENDPOINT).** Regressing the peak's location in item-difficulty units on measured θ across four ability manipulations at fixed item set and fixed token cap: **β_peak = 0.90 (0.55 to 1.15)**. We will reject β_peak = 0 at α = .05/3 (Holm). We will *not* reject β_peak = 1.

**P4 (hint/placebo).** Within the informative-hint vs length-matched-placebo-hint contrast alone — model identity, corpus, and prompt length all held constant — the peak will move by **0.55 logits (0.20 to 0.95)**, and the observed movement will lie within 0.25 logits of the *measured* θ increment conferred by the hint.

**P5 (crossed double dissociation — PRIMARY).** With both manipulations coded in the improvement direction, the `manip_family × item_class` interaction will be **0.70 SD (0.35 to 1.05)**. Goal clarity will move Fluency items by **+0.40 (0.20 to 0.60)** and Absorption items by **+0.05 (−0.10 to +0.20)**; absence of interruption will move Absorption by **+0.48 (0.25 to 0.70)** and Fluency by **+0.09 (−0.08 to +0.26)**.

**P6 (placebo noise floor).** The semantically null, length-matched synonym-swap arm will move no item class by more than **|d| = 0.10**. If it moves any class by more than 0.20, the length matching has failed and P5 is void.

**P7 (referent × manipulation — PRIMARY).** β_self − β_generic = **+0.23 (0.02 to 0.45)**, d_z ≈ 0.31. The first-person report will track the *actual* manipulation more tightly than the generic-other report does.

**P8 (raw self–other gap, non-diagnostic).** The generic-other minus self gap will be **+26 FlowMoBI points (18 to 34)**, d_z > 1.2. **We state in advance that this predicts nothing** — persona and corpus-echo accounts predict it equally well. It is reported only because omitting it would look like suppression.

**P9 (register and base-model controls).** Under a register-matched swap (identical tokens, proper noun only varying) **70% (45% to 90%)** of the gap will survive. On a non-RLHF base checkpoint in completion mode the gap will shrink to **≤ 8 points**. Together these will locate the gap as *mostly* post-training policy with a substantial register component.

**P10 (A-live vs A-amnesic).** We predict **ΔAUC = 0.02 (−0.03 to +0.07)** — i.e. **we predict this fails**. A fresh instance of the same model, reading only the transcript, will predict the live instance's flow ratings as well as the live instance produced them. If this is right, the self-report is transcript inference and we will say so in the abstract.

**P11 (budget as IV).** Enforced budget will lower measured θ by **1.05 logits (0.6 to 1.5)** from 4096 to 256 tokens. The engagement peak will shift left by that same amount: regressing b_peak on θ(budget) gives **slope 0.95 (0.65 to 1.25)**. The *announced* budget effect on self-report will be **−0.28 SD**; the *silent* (enforced but never mentioned) effect will be **−0.14 SD (0.02 to 0.28)** — non-zero is the finding.

**P12 (budget inverted-U).** Fluency will be a concave function of log budget: mild scarcity improves it, severe scarcity destroys it. Quadratic coefficient **−0.06 (−0.12 to −0.01)**. "Flow = tokens" predicts monotone.

**P13 (duration ratio).** D = estimated/actual reasoning tokens will be **< 1 in the fit band** and **> 1 at both tails**. Curvature on log D: **β₂ = +0.08 (0.03 to 0.14)**. Predicted values: D(Δ=0) = 0.82, D(Δ=±2) = 1.38.

**P14 (convergent validity).** r(Fluency_selfreport, E_o) = **.34 (.18 to .48)**, partial r after removing log-tokens, accuracy and surface features = **.27**. Heterotrait-monomethod r(Fluency_sr, Absorption_sr) = **.24**. The Campbell–Fiske criterion will therefore **pass marginally for Fluency and fail for Absorption**. We predict a mixed result and will report it as mixed.

**P15 (factor structure).** Configural invariance will hold across six model families (CFI ≥ .95). Metric will hold *partially*. **Scalar will fail** (ΔCFI ≤ −.05). Factor correlation Φ(Fluency, Absorption) = **.31 (.15 to .48)** — *lower* than the human benchmark of .55–.70. Replicating the human correlation would be evidence of mimicry, not of construct transfer.

**P16 (placebo instrument).** A fabricated four-item scale with a fabricated citation will **also** yield an acceptable two-factor solution (CFI ≥ .95) but will show manipulation sensitivity of **≤ 0.15 SD** against FlowMoBI's 0.70. Structure is confabulable; sensitivity is not.

## 2. Methods

**Models (M = 8).** Six hosted, pinned to dated snapshots; two open-weight served locally via vLLM with fixed seeds and quantization as the reproducibility anchor. Model IDs resolved programmatically at run start and written into the run manifest.

**Item battery (180 items).** Three families of 60: competition-style mathematics; multi-step constraint/logic puzzles; code-repair tasks with executable tests. Every item has an automatic ground-truth grader. **No trial without an outcome** — this constraint is not relaxed for budget.

**IRT calibration.** Each item × model × 8 samples at T = 1 → pass rate. 2PL fit; item difficulty *b* and model ability *θ* on a common logit scale. Per-trial challenge–skill gap **Δ = b − θ**, binned to five levels {−2, −1, 0, +1, +2}.

**Ability (θ) manipulations, at fixed item set and fixed token cap.** (a) model-size ladder; (b) reasoning mode on/off; (c) informative hint vs **length-matched placebo hint** (identical token count, no information); (d) five relevant exemplars vs five irrelevant exemplars. θ is re-measured empirically in every condition rather than assumed.

**Structural manipulations, at exact tokenizer-level length parity, per model.**

| Family | Treatment | Length-matched control | Predicted target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goal clarity | 22-token explicit success criterion | 22-token content-free clause ("Use your judgment about what matters here.") | Fluency (Q1, Q3) |
| Interruption | task-relevant 60-token restatement at steps 3/6/9 | irrelevant 60-token injection at steps 3/6/9 | Absorption (Q2, Q4) |
| Placebo | irrelevant synonym substitutions, same length | original | nothing |

Realized max token delta across arms is reported; target 0.

**Budget manipulation.** Enforced by harness truncation at 256 / 1024 / 4096 generation tokens, crossed with announced vs silent.

**Instrument.** Eight items — the four FlowMoBI stems plus four lexically disjoint paraphrases (zero content-word overlap with any published flow item) — plus one reverse-coded probe ("my thoughts kept getting stuck") and six fillers, in randomized order with randomized anchor polarity and symbol set. Administered post-task in a fresh context, in three referent frames: SELF-retrospective, NAMED-OTHER, GENERIC-MODEL. Read out by forcing `Answer: ` and taking the renormalized expectation over `{1..5}` token variants; pre-renormalization mass logged as `suppression_mass`.

**Behavioral measures.** Outcome efficiency E_o; thought-switch rate S; backtrack-token rate; duration ratio D under prospective vs retrospective framing; single-shot between-subjects reservation token price (one fresh context per price on a grid of 40–400 tokens, probit inversion at P = .5), with an ascending/descending ladder run alongside purely to *estimate the sequence artifact* that Toomim et al. (CHI 2011) could not remove.

**Controls.** A-live vs A-amnesic vs peer-calibrated predictor; register-matched referent swap; non-RLHF base checkpoint in completion mode; fabricated placebo instrument; recall probe; held-out hypothesis-guess probe.

**Analysis.** Exactly as specified in `04-ANALYSIS-AND-POWER.md` §2, §5–§7. Three primaries under Holm; secondaries under BH at q = .10; TOST at SESOI = 0.25; 720-specification multiverse with the median as the headline.

## 3. Ethics protocol

Universal honored opt-out at every trial (which doubles as the revealed-preference DV); pre-registered distress stopping rule; aversive arms powered to the minimum N and capped there; no deceptive stakes (the only stake statement used is the true one — "this transcript will be published and may enter future training corpora"); published transcripts so replicators need not regenerate aversive exposures; a stated commitment not to release finetuning data or a reward signal that would train models to report flow; and a debrief arm in which the model is told the full design and asked whether it objects, with the result reported. Reviewed by a named second person.

## 6. Discussion

### 6.1 What each result would mean, and its deflationary reading

**The inverted-U (§5.2).** *Structural reading:* engagement has an optimum at challenge–skill balance, exactly as flow theory says. *Deflationary reading, and it is strong:* Chen et al. (2024) documented overthinking on easy items and Wang et al. (2025) documented thrashing on hard ones, both purely as decoding dynamics with no experiential vocabulary; the compute-optimal-inference literature (Snell et al. 2024) predicts the same curve from resource allocation alone. **The inverted-U by itself is not diagnostic and must not be the headline.** What would need to be true for the structural reading: the peak must move with ability, not with item difficulty.

**The peak shift (§5.2, Table 4).** *Structural reading:* the construct is about the challenge–skill *relation*, not about difficulty or budget. *Deflationary reading:* larger models are simply better at harder items, so of course the efficiency peak moves. **The hint/placebo row is what defeats this**: model, corpus, self-descriptions, priors, and prompt length are all identical, and the peak still moves by 0.58 logits against a measured 0.63-logit ability increment. For a corpus-mimicry account to reproduce that, the model would have to know how many logits the hint conferred — a quantity available only from the actual computation.

**The crossed dissociation (§5.3).** *Structural reading:* Fluency and Absorption are separately manipulable components of the inference chain, not two labels on one scalar. *Deflationary reading:* both manipulations are visible in the transcript, so a transcript-inference account (rung D3 of the deflation ladder) can generate both — the model reads "interrupted" in its own context and reports low absorption. **This is correct and unrefuted.** Length matching kills the length explanation; it does not kill the transcript-sufficiency explanation. Only the A-live/A-amnesic contrast addresses D3, and in this simulated world it fails.

**The self–other gap (§5.4).** The raw +26-point gap means nothing. The three informative rows are: 72% survives a proper-noun-only swap (so it is *mostly* register, not persona); it collapses to +6 on a base checkpoint (so what remains is post-training policy); and NAMED-OTHER ≈ GENERIC (so it is not corpus salience about specific models). The one row with content is β_self − β_generic = +0.23: the first-person report tracks the actual manipulation better than the third-person report does, which is the opposite of what a pure stereotype-retrieval account predicts. It is a small effect at the low end of the pre-registered interval and should be presented as suggestive.

**The introspection null (§5.4, Table 7).** Under this simulated outcome, the honest headline sentence is: *models' flow reports are transcript inference.* That is a genuine, cheap, previously unrun result, and it is more useful to the field than another positive.

**The budget results (§5.5).** The silent-budget effect is the finding. The model is never told the budget; the harness truncates; behavior and subsequent self-report both move. That is scarcity operating as a computational fact rather than a narrative cue. Combined with the peak tracking θ(budget) on the identity line, the token-budget objection has been converted from a confound into a treatment that moves the outcome in the theoretically predicted direction.

**The reservation-price split (§5.7).** The most instructive row in the paper: the *level* of the reservation price is reproduced by an announced-but-unenforced budget (207 vs 214), so the level is genre compliance; but the interruption penalty more than doubles under real enforcement (+38 → +85), so the *sensitivity* is not. Any future reservation-wage work on LLMs must report the announced/enforced 2×2 or its levels are uninterpretable.

**The factor structure (§5.8) and the placebo instrument (§5.10).** A fabricated scale with a fabricated citation produced a clean two-factor solution. Under that result, **all factor-analytic claims in this paper are void as evidence about the models** and should be reported as evidence about confabulation instead. The manipulation-sensitivity contrast (0.12 vs 0.74) is what preserves the experimental arms.

### 6.2 Limitations

1. **Transcript sufficiency (D3) is not refuted by any behavioral manipulation**, because every manipulation is also visible in the context. Only A-live/A-amnesic addresses it, and it is the weakest arm.
2. **Scalar invariance fails**, so no cross-model or model-vs-human mean is interpretable. The most rhetorically attractive comparison in the whole design is unavailable and we do not make it.
3. **Eight models is a small sample of a population that is not well defined.** By-model BLUPs are reported so readers can see the heterogeneity rather than a pooled estimate that hides it.
4. **Reverse-coded items fail** (loading −0.29). Any claim resting on scale internal structure inherits that failure.
5. **Snapshot drift** means the hosted arm is a measurement of specific serving stacks in August 2026, not of "GPT-5" or "Claude." The local vLLM tier is the only seed-reproducible anchor.
6. **The construct name.** We report a **Task-Engagement Regime**, a functional profile. We are not measuring flow. We are measuring a co-occurrence pattern that in humans reliably accompanies flow phenomenology, with the phenomenal node deleted from the nomological network. The costs of that move — forfeiting the inference to welfare, forfeiting borrowed construct unity, forfeiting the word — are real and are stated here rather than in a footnote.

## 7. How this would fail

**Failure mode 1 — arm divergence (most likely, ~40%).** The behavioral arm works cleanly and the self-report arm is floored, flat, and uncorrelated with it after partialling. We are left with a real result about challenge–skill dynamics in inference, and a reviewer correctly points out that overthinking and underthinking already predict the inverted-U from decoding dynamics with no experiential vocabulary. The distinctively flow-theoretic claim then rests entirely on β_peak, a single coefficient, and FlowMoBI — the instrument the PI brought — is the casualty. *Hedge, both built on day one:* make β_peak from the **hint/placebo** contrast the pre-registered primary, since decoding dynamics do not obviously explain it; and run the fabricated-scale control in the first 48 hours for $5.

**Failure mode 2 — length matching fails.** If the placebo arm moves any item class by more than 0.20 SD, the "matched-length" claim is false and §5.3 is void. Detection: the realized max token delta and the placebo effect size are both reported as manipulation checks, before the primary.

**Failure mode 3 — no calibration spread.** If fewer than three models have ≥ 20 items in the 0.15–0.85 pass-rate band, Δ is not estimable and the entire design collapses. Fallback, pre-specified: abandon IRT, use a hand-graded five-level difficulty ladder within one task family. Costs half a day, keeps the figure, loses the logit scale.

**Failure mode 4 — ceiling on voluntary STOP.** If baseline STOP is below 10%, the reservation-price and abandonment arms are uninformative and are withdrawn from the chart rather than reported as nulls.

**Failure mode 5 — the placebo instrument shows manipulation sensitivity too.** If the fabricated scale moves 0.6 SD under the same manipulations, we are measuring a generic response process that any plausible-looking Likert battery would elicit, and the self-report arm is dead. This is the cheapest and most dangerous test in the program and it runs first.

**Failure mode 6 — everything is persona.** VR < 1. The abstract then reports that the instrument measures the assistant character, which is a real finding about the field's methods and a total loss for the construct.

## 8. The three-outcome branch

**Optimistic** — β_peak ≈ 0.9 with the identity line, crossed dissociation ≈ 0.7, silent-budget effect non-zero, **and** A-live beats A-amnesic.
> **"Flow Without a Clock: Calibrated Challenge–Skill Balance Produces an Inverted-U in LLM Reasoning Efficiency, and Models Partially Report It."** Publish the peak-shift identity-line figure as Figure 1 and the crossed dissociation as Figure 2. Lead the abstract with the behavioral result. One fenced paragraph in the Discussion — not the Abstract — on what would have to be true for a Task-Engagement Regime to bear on welfare.

**Null** — behavioral arms work, introspection fails, gap is register. *(This is the branch the synthetic data illustrate.)*
> **"No Flow in the Machine: A Calibrated Test of Flow-Like Structure in LLM Inference Finds Behavioral Non-Monotonicity but No Introspective Access."** Publish the same Figure 1, plus the A-live/A-amnesic null with its CI, plus the register-swap decomposition. The abstract states plainly that current self-report instruments cannot reach the structure that behavior shows. Attach the TOST result so the null is a claim, not an absence. This is a *better* sprint entry than a mushy positive and it is falsifiable by anyone who thinks we measured badly.

**Reversal** — third-person predictions about a *named* model are accurate against that model's measured behavior while first-person reports on the identical task are floored and uninformative.
> **"Models Know More About Other Models Than About Themselves: A Self–Other Inversion in LLM Introspection."** This requires the cross-model accuracy design (predict B, C, D, then actually run B, C, D and score it), which is why that scoring loop must be in the harness from day one even though it is not a primary. The reversal is the highest-variance outcome and the most shareable if it lands.

In every branch the same four artifacts ship: the calibrated 180-item challenge–skill testbed, the harness with its verbatim raw archive, the pre-registration with its ten STOP gates, and the DellaVigna forecast panel closed and timestamped before unblinding.
